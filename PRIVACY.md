# Privacy Policy for Tru Construction Estimator

**Last updated: May 2026**

David King ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Tru Construction Estimator mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Tru Construction Estimator is a privacy-first application.** We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information on our servers.

All data created in the App — including projects, clients, settings, and quotes — is stored **locally on your device only** using SQLite. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it (for example, by emailing a generated PDF quote to a client).

### Data You Enter Into the App
The following data is stored locally on your device and is NOT transmitted to us:
- Project names, descriptions, and locations
- Client names, phone numbers, emails, and addresses
- Cost calculations and quotes
- Company information you enter in Settings

### Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do not use analytics, tracking, or telemetry
- We do not show advertisements
- We do not share data with third parties
- We do not track your location
- We do not access your contacts, photos, or other device data without your explicit action

## 2. PDF Generation

When you generate a PDF quote in the App:
- The PDF is created on your device
- The PDF is shared via your device's standard sharing mechanism (email, messaging, AirDrop, etc.)
- We do not have access to or store the PDF
- It is your responsibility to ensure you have permission to share any client information included in the PDF

## 3. Subscription Information

If you subscribe to Tru Construction Estimator Pro:
- Subscription processing is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play Store
- We do not receive your payment information
- We only receive notification that you have subscribed
- You may cancel your subscription at any time through your App Store or Play Store account

## 4. Children's Privacy

Tru Construction Estimator is intended for use by professional adults (construction contractors). We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this application.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

## 6. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal information on our servers, there is no data for us to delete or modify. To remove your data:
- Delete the app from your device, OR
- Use the app's built-in delete features for individual items

For users in the European Union (GDPR) or California (CCPA): You retain full control over your data because it remains exclusively on your device.
