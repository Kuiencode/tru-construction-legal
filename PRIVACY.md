# Privacy Policy for Tru Construction Estimator

**Last updated: October 2026**

David King ("we," "our," or "us") operates Tru Construction Estimator (the "Service"), a web app at tru-estimator.com that can also be installed on your phone or computer. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account information.** Your email address, your name, your password (stored in scrambled form, never in plain text), your role (contractor or client), and your profile photo if you add one.

**Work you save in the Service.** Projects and estimates (including line items and notes), clients and their contact details (names, phone numbers, emails, addresses), invoices and payments, quotes, work orders, change orders, expenses, time entries, site logs, photos, documents, signatures, saved calculations, and your business details and settings (such as your company name, logo, region and terms).

**Public listings.** If you create a contractor listing, your business name, location, trades, photos and reviews are shown publicly in the contractor directory. Quote requests and reviews you submit are visible to the people they are addressed to.

**Usage information.** We record the days you open the app and the last time you were active. We do not record which pages you visit or what you click. We also record when a quote, work order or other link you shared is opened.

**Technical information.** Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the time of a request) to keep the Service running and secure.

**Cookies and storage.** We use a sign-in cookie to keep you logged in, and your browser's local storage to remember preferences. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

**Payment information.** Paid plans are processed by Lemon Squeezy. We receive your subscription status, but not your card number.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To provide the Service: to store your work, create quotes and invoices, and show your listing.
- To keep your account secure and to prevent misuse.
- To contact you about your account, such as password resets, service notices, and replies to your questions.
- To understand how many people use the Service, so we can improve it.
- To provide support. Our administrators can view account details and the work stored in an account when needed to run and support the Service.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertisements.

## 3. Who Handles Your Information

We use trusted providers to run the Service. They process information only to provide their service to us:

- **Supabase**: database, sign-in and file storage
- **Vercel**: website hosting
- **Resend**: sending and receiving email, such as password resets and messages from us. Replies to our emails are received at team@tru-estimator.com.
- **Lemon Squeezy**: payments and subscriptions

These providers may process information on servers outside Jamaica, including in the United States.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the Service.

**Your clients' information.** When you enter information about your own clients, you are responsible for having the right to do so. When you share a quote or invoice link, the person who opens it can see what is in that document.

## 4. Email

We send emails needed to run your account, such as password resets, and messages from us about your account or the Service. If we ever send promotional emails, we will include a way to opt out.

## 5. How Long We Keep Information

We keep your information while your account is active. If your account is deleted, we remove your login, your projects, clients, invoices and your uploaded files. We keep occasional backup copies for disaster recovery, and information in those copies is removed when the backups are replaced.

## 6. Security

Information is encrypted while it travels between you and the Service, and access to each account's data is restricted to that account's owner and our administrators. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

You can view and edit most of your information in the app. You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, or delete your account and its data. To do so, email us at the address below from the email you signed up with. We will respond within a reasonable time.

We aim to handle personal data in line with the laws that apply to us, including Jamaica's Data Protection Act, 2020. If you live somewhere else with data protection rights, such as the European Union or California, you can make the same requests and we will respond as the law requires.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for adults who work in or hire for construction. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and we will email you about important changes.
